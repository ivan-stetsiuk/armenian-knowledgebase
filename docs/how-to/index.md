# How-to

Ready scripts for a situation: what to say, what you will hear back, what to do if you get stuck. No grammar explanations here, only links to them. Every line comes from the lessons.

- [Introduce yourself](introduce-yourself.md) — name, where from, age, what you speak.
- [Greetings and farewells](greetings.md) — hello, good morning, goodbye, see you.
- [How are you](how-are-you.md) — the question and twenty answers.
- [Ask the day and the date](ask-the-day-and-date.md) — what day, what date, when is your birthday.
- [Tell the time](tell-the-time.md) — what time is it, at what time.
- [Talk about the weather](talk-about-weather.md) — hot, cold, raining, the temperature.
- [At the table](at-the-table.md) — meals, coffee with or without, pass the salt.
- [Give and ask directions](give-directions.md) — straight, left, right, next to, across from.

## When you are stuck

| Armenian | English |
|---|---|
| Նորից ասա։ | Say it again. |
| Մի րոպե մտածեմ։ | Let me think for a second. |
| Վստահ չեմ։ | I am not sure. |
| Գաղափար չունեմ։ | No idea. |
| Ճիշտն ասած… / Անկեղծ ասած… | Honestly speaking… |
| Մի խոսքով… | In short… (короче) |
