## Exercises

### Exercise 1 · Translate the following sentences into Armenian

1. There are two chairs in the living room.
2. I have a small table in the kitchen.
3. There are three pillows on the sofa.
4. There is a bed in the bedroom.
5. The dining room's curtains are white.
6. The refrigerator is full (լիքը).
7. The closet is empty (դատարկ).
8. The cat is in the armchair.
9. There is a carpet on the floor.
10. There is a mirror in the bathroom.
11. There is a clock on the wall.
12. The balcony is very big.
