---
title: "Simple past (aorist)"
---

> **One line.** A one-word past with personal endings and no auxiliary: վճարել → Վճարեցի — I paid; խմել → Ես խմեցի — I drank, Քանի՞ հատ խմեցիր։ — How many did you drink?

## Formation

Covered only in passing so far; the paradigm has not yet been taught. The forms that have come up:

| Infinitive | Simple past | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| վճարել — to pay | Վճարեցի | I paid ("true past — the hard one", lesson 20) |
| խմել — to drink | Ես խմեցի · Քանի՞ հատ նեգրոնի խմեցիր։ | I drank · How many negronis did you drink? |
| աշխատել — to work | Քանի՞ ժամ աշխատեցիր։ — Հինգ ժամ։ | How many hours did you work? — Five hours. |
| քայլել — to walk | Նա քայլեց այգու միջով, որ տուն հասնի։ | She walked through the park to get home. |
| անցնել — to pass | Ո՞նց անցավ։ | How did it go? |
| մնալ — to stay | Արևին կարոտ մնացինք։ | We missed the sun (lit. we stayed longing for the sun). |

What can be read off these: -ել verbs take -եցի / -եցիր / -եց (խմեցի, խմեցիր, քայլեց); -ալ verbs take -ացի… (մնացինք); անցնել drops its -ն- (անցավ), which is the rule below.

## Usage

- Վճարեցի։ — I paid.
- Ես խմեցի։ — I drank.
- Ո՞նց անցավ։ — How did it go?

## Notes and exceptions

- **Derivative verbs** (suffix -ան, -են, -ն, -չ before -ալ / -ել) drop the -ն / -չ in the simple past, just as in the imperative and the perfect: անցնել → անցավ. See [Verbs in -անալ / -ենալ](/grammar/verbs-anal/) and [Imperative](/grammar/imperative/).
- Do not confuse with the perfect, which keeps the auxiliary: Աննայի մայրիկին ավտոմեքենան է խփել — a car has hit Anna's mother ([Perfect](/grammar/perfect/)); or with the imperfect "was doing" ([Past of to be](/grammar/past-of-to-be/)).
- The tutor set "Past Simple" as a homework topic in lesson 20 and a lesson on it was cancelled; expect a full paradigm later.
