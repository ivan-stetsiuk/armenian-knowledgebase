## Grammar

::::rule{title="Postpositions follow a noun in the genitive"}

**The postposition comes after its noun, and the noun stands in the genitive (-ի, -ու, -ան…); pronouns use their possessive form (իմ կողքին, մեր միջև).**

:::example
Պայուսակը սեղանի վրա է։ — The bag is on the table.  
Նա քայլեց այգու միջով, որ տուն հասնի։ — She walked through the park to get home.  
Այշեն միշտ նստում է իմ կողքին։ — Ayshe always sits next to me.
:::

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 · Translate into Armenian

1. Put your books and newspapers on my or my friend's table.
2. Do you know where my dog is? — Yeah, your dog is under the big yellow chair.
3. There is a cafe next to the supermarket. Can we meet there tomorrow morning?
4. Can she put her apples in your big bag?
5. We are going to walk around the Republic Square next week, do you want to join us? — The dog was running around us.
6. There is no diplomatic relationship (դիվանագիտական հարաբերություն) between Armenia and Turkey.
7. Look up and you will see her.
8. Where is Jane's son? He is upstairs.
9. Your friends are downstairs waiting for you.
10. Where is my coffee? It is next to the computer.
11. They will wait for you across from the hospital.
12. There is a big park behind the hotel.
13. When will you get here? I am in front of your building.
14. The woman is passing through (միջով) the Red Sea.
15. They will go through (միջով) the forest and then through the small town.
